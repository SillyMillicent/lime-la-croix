# general notes
- almost all of the big, important hits are physical damage *except* for the quadra/octo dive itself and the self fuse bomb explosions
- pretend your fuse for fusefield is a hold instead of a tap. mentally changing how you soak this fuse will increase your consistency.
- if you use knockback resistance for bombarian special, it will not be up for the next knockback quadra/octo dive
